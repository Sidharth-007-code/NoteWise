## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an intellectual sanctuary—an environment evoking the stillness, tactile warmth, and focused dignity of an archival reading room fused with precise modern cataloging. Designed for university researchers and students, the interface cultivates sustained attention, contemplation, and academic rigour.

The design movement is an **Editorial Flat UI with Restrained Soft-Skeuomorphic Touches**. Surfaces behave like fine, archival uncoated stock: flat, crisp, and tactile without overt faux-textures. Structural definition relies on delicate hairline dividers reminiscent of bookbinder rules, layered paper elevations, and gentle two-layer shadows. Synthetic gradients, electric neon accents, floating cards with high blur, and generic dashboard widgets are strictly excluded. The emotional cadence is scholarly, tranquil, grounded, and authoritative.

## Colors

The palette reproduces the materials of an archival scholar's desk: aged vellum, dark bookcloth, warm leather binding, and patinated sage desk lamps.

- **Primary (`#293A34` - Deep Forest Slate):** Anchors navigation, global headers, primary action states, and high-emphasis structure.
- **Secondary (`#7E5A44` - Warm Leather Accent):** Governs interactive inline text, citations, textual anchors, and highlighted metadata.
- **Tertiary (`#A5B69C` - Pale Patina Sage):** Restricted strictly to solid fills, completion rings, bookmarked ribbons, and active state highlights. Never use as text over light surfaces.
- **Neutral Primary (`#292B29` - Carbon Ink):** Emulates archival ink for high-contrast, effortless legibility across all long-form academic text.
- **Neutral Secondary (`#5E625C` - Washed Ink):** Subdued contrast for captions, citation sources, timestamps, and secondary metadata.
- **Canvas Base (`#F5F1E8` - Unbleached Parchment):** The global viewport background providing warm, low-glare reading endurance.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFEFA` - Smooth Vellum):** Distinct reading surfaces, open book plates, notebooks, and floating study panes.
- **Border / Rule (`#D8CDBA` - Folio Rule):** Precise hairline delimiters and panel separation.

## Typography

The typography system establishes a distinct division between editorial presence, utilitarian navigation, immersive reading, and structured data.

- **Headings (Playfair Display):** Conveys traditional academic pedigree and editorial dignity. Applied to page titles, document headers, book chapters, and research queries. Headings must always maintain relaxed leading and avoid tight line clamping.
- **UI & Interface Labels (DM Sans):** Low-contrast geometric clarity designed for effortless navigation, sidebars, metadata tags, button labels, and operational prompts.
- **Long-Form Reading (Source Serif 4):** Reserved for uninterrupted study—abstracts, essay reader views, extracted transcripts, and AI-synthesized research papers. Optical line heights sit comfortably between 1.65 and 1.8 to prevent visual fatigue.
- **Technical & Citations (JetBrains Mono):** Monospaced precision for inline code, raw citations, DOI indicators, data tables, and archival index identifiers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model mimics the proportions of a broadsheet academic review or a twin-page folio. The grid follows a 12-column structure for desktop reading dashboards, reflowing into an asymmetric dual-pane layout for research reading, and a single vertical column for mobile study modes.

- **Desktop (>= 1200px):** 12-column grid with `3rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Maximum content reading containers must not exceed `72ch` to preserve optical legibility.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column grid with `2rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Collapsible contextual metadata drawers.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid with `1.25rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Stacked single-column reading with sticky contextual anchors.

Whitespace is deliberately generous and architectural, functioning as a silent framing device rather than vacant space. Structural divisions must rely primarily on whitespace intervals and hairline rules rather than heavy boxed enclosures.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through paper layering and directional, warm-tinted micro-shadows that evoke thick, deckled-edge paper laid over a study desk.

- **Flat Substrate (Canvas):** `#F5F1E8` holds no elevation and serves as the structural floor.
- **Layer 1 (Resting Folios / Content Panes):** `#FFFEFA` sits flat atop the canvas, defined entirely by a crisp 1px border of `#D8CDBA` without shadow.
- **Layer 2 (Lifted Documents / Focused Study Slates):** Two-layer shadow consisting of an immediate micro-contact shadow and an ambient soft penumbra:
  `box-shadow: 0 1px 2px rgba(41, 43, 41, 0.04), 0 4px 12px rgba(126, 90, 68, 0.06);`
  accompanied by a 1px border in `#D8CDBA`.
- **Layer 3 (Modals / Contextual Lexicons / Floating Annotations):** Elevated reading palette:
  `box-shadow: 0 2px 4px rgba(41, 43, 41, 0.04), 0 12px 28px rgba(41, 58, 52, 0.08);`
  with a 1px border in `#D8CDBA`.
- **Dividers & Structural Rules:** Continuous 1px solids using `#D8CDBA`. Avoid dotted borders or heavy drop shadows.

## Shapes

Shapes emulate trimmed manuscript sheets, card catalog indexes, and hardbound cloth spine edges. Curves are minimal and restrained (`roundedness: 1`).

- **Default Element Radius (`0.25rem` / `4px`):** Used across input controls, contextual chips, inline blocks, book spine markers, and standard buttons.
- **Structural Plate Radius (`rounded-lg: 0.5rem` / `8px`):** Applied strictly to main reading plates, floating modal panels, and study split-views.
- **Inner Accents:** Internal badges and bookmark tags maintain crisp, near-square corners to evoke hand-cut book plates.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#293A34` fill, `#FFFEFA` text in `label-md` (DM Sans), `0.25rem` radius, padded with `0.625rem 1.25rem`. On hover, color transitions slightly deeper to `#1E2B26`. Never use drop shadows on buttons.
- **Secondary / Scholarly Action:** `#FFFEFA` surface with 1px border in `#D8CDBA`, text in `#292B29`. Hover transitions background to `#F5F1E8`.
- **Text Action / Citation Trigger:** No background or border. Text styled in `#7E5A44` with a subtle, 1px bottom border spaced `2px` below the baseline.

### Chips & Academic Tags
- **Metadata Badges:** Background `#F5F1E8`, 1px border `#D8CDBA`, text `#5E625C` in `label-sm`.
- **Active Filter / Topic Chip:** Background `#293A34`, text `#FFFEFA`, no border.
- **Archival Category Pill:** Background `#A5B69C` with `#293A34` text, applied sparingly to indicate verified library sources or active research paths.

### Lists & Catalog Indexes
- Strip all container boxes. Rows are separated by 1px hairline rules in `#D8CDBA`.
- Left-aligned metadata identifier set in `code-md` (`#5E625C`), primary title in `headline-sm` or `body-md` (`#292B29`), and citation metrics right-aligned in `DM Sans`.
- Row hover produces an ambient wash of `#FFFEFA`.

### Checkboxes & Selection Inputs
- Square with `2px` micro-radius. 1px border in `#D8CDBA` against `#FFFEFA` background.
- Selected state fills with `#293A34` featuring an off-white `#FFFEFA` checkmark.

### Form & Search Fields
- Inset or underlined style echoing archival catalogs. Surface is `#FFFEFA` with a perimeter 1px border in `#D8CDBA`.
- Active focus state sharpens the border to `#293A34` without an outer glow. Placeholder text in `#5E625C`.

### Reading Folios & Study Panels (Replacing SaaS Cards)
- Reject card paradigms (no heavy multi-colored borders, no pill tags piled high, no floating avatars).
- Structure content as a "Folio": surface `#FFFEFA`, bounded by a single hairline border in `#D8CDBA`, with top header zones demarked by a single horizontal rule.
- Padding inside folios is spacious (`space-lg` to `space-xl`).

### AI Study Companion & Annotation Callouts
- Marginalia or synthesis blocks render with a left accent border: 3px solid `#7E5A44` or `#A5B69C`.
- Background remains pure `#FFFEFA` or shifts to a delicate tint `#F5F1E8`. Text displays in `reading-md` (Source Serif 4) for synthesis summaries and `body-sm` (DM Sans) for prompt metadata.
- Research progress bars utilize `#A5B69C` fill on an unfilled track of `#D8CDBA`.
