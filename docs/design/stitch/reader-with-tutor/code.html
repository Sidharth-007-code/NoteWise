Section 3.2 Core Analytical Lemma

Theorem 3.2: Conditional Invariance of the Exponential Distribution

Formal proof of the continuous memoryless characteristic and preservation of survivor probability under translation.

Let $X$ be a continuous random variable having support over the non-negative real numbers $[0, \infty)$. The random variable is distributed exponentially with parameter $\lambda > 0$, denoted as $X \sim \text{Exp}(\lambda)$, possessing probability density function $f(x) = \lambda e^{-\lambda x}$.

STATEMENT OF THEOREM 3.2 (Memoryless Property)

For any strictly positive scalar constants $s, t > 0$, the conditional probability that the random process persists beyond duration $s + t$, conditioned upon surviving elapsed threshold $s$, depends exclusively upon duration $t$:

P(X > s + t | X > s) = P(X > t)

Analytical Derivation & Proof

By the elementary definition of conditional probability for continuous cumulative distribution envelopes:

DERIVATION STEPS EQ. 3.2.4

P(X > s + t | X > s) = P({X > s + t} ∩ {X > s}) / P(X > s)

Since {X > s + t} ⊆ {X > s}, the joint intersection simplifies strictly to {X > s + t}:

= P(X > s + t) / P(X > s)

Recalling the complementary cumulative survival distribution S(x) = e^(-λx):

= e^(-λ(s + t)) / e^(-λs)

= e^(-λs) · e^(-λt) / e^(-λs)

= e^(-λt) = P(X > t) Q.E.D. ∎

In reliability engineering contexts, this algebraic closure dictates that an operational component which has survived $s$ operational hours functions mathematically as if brand new with regards to its remaining lifespan expectancy.
